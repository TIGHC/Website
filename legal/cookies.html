No tracking cookies

tighc.stuxie.dev sets no cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics of any kind. There are no third-party ad networks, no analytics scripts, and no tracking pixels anywhere on the site.

What we actually store

The only browser storage this site uses is localStorage — distinct from cookies, and never sent to any server the way a cookie is. It holds exactly one thing:

  • tighc-age-confirmed — set once you click through the 18+ notice, so it doesn't show again on your next visit.

That's stored entirely on your device and can be cleared any time via your browser's site data settings — clearing it just brings the 18+ notice back.

GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may set its own cookies or perform its own logging as part of serving the site, outside of anything TIGHC controls — see GitHub's privacy statement for that.

Consent management

There's no cookie consent banner here because nothing on this site legally requires one — there are no non-essential cookies to ask permission for.
