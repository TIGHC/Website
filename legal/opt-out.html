No data sale or sharing

Laws like California's CCPA/CPRA give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. TIGHC has nothing to opt out of in that sense — this site and the Engine software don't collect personal data for advertising or analytics, so there's none to sell or share.

What actually happens

The only outside data flow is your own browser fetching public content directly from GitHub (profiles, changelogs, version numbers — see the Privacy Policy) or, if you opt in inside the Engine, your own SteamGridDB API key fetching cover art. Neither is something TIGHC could sell even if it wanted to — we're not in the middle of either request.

Do Not Track

Because this site runs no tracking scripts, a browser's "Do Not Track" signal has no additional effect here — there's nothing being tracked either way.

Questions

If you have questions about data handling not covered here, open an issue on GitHub or email legal@stuxie.dev.
